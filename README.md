# UnfoldMap — Privacy Policy

The privacy policy for **UnfoldMap**, a visionOS research demo distributed
through TestFlight.

Published at <https://yourack-anamor.github.io/unfoldmap-privacy/>.

This repository holds one page and nothing else. It is public because a privacy
policy has to be readable without an account — by TestFlight testers, by App
Review, and by anyone who reads about the app before installing it.

The source of truth lives with the app, at `docs/legal/privacy-policy.html` in
the app repository. **Edit it there first,** then copy it here — otherwise the
page and the app drift apart, and the page is the half people trust.
